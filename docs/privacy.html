ScoreBook Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2, 2026

TL;DR: ScoreBook stores all your data locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. Period.

Data Collection

ScoreBook does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. All tournament data — including player names, scores, and standings — is stored exclusively on your device using a local database (SQLite).

Data Storage

  • All data is stored locally on your device using expo-sqlite.
  • No data is uploaded to any cloud service or external server.
  • Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all stored data.

Sharing Features

The app includes a "Share Standings" feature that lets you export an image of the leaderboard. When you use this feature:

  • The image is generated entirely on your device.
  • Sharing uses your device's native share sheet (Android sharing dialog).
  • No data is sent to us — you choose where to share it.

Permissions

ScoreBook requests the following Android permissions:

  • Internet access — required by the framework for app functionality. No data is transmitted to external servers.

No other permissions (camera, contacts, location, storage) are requested or used.

Third-Party Services

ScoreBook does not use any third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, crash reporting services, or social login. There are no external data processors involved.

Children's Privacy

ScoreBook is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Data Retention

All data persists on your device until you delete it within the app or uninstall the app. We retain no data server-side because we collect none.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
